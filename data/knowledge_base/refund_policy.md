# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund according to the company's refund rules and the condition of the order.

## Refund Request

A customer can contact support to request a refund. The support system can provide information about the refund policy.

## Refund Processing

After a refund request is reviewed and approved, the refund is processed through the applicable payment method.

## Refund Status

Customers can contact support if they need information about the status of a refund.

## Support Ticket

If a refund issue cannot be resolved through the available information, the system can create a support ticket for further assistance.
