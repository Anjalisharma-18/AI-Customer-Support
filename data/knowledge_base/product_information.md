# Product Information

## Products

Our company provides products that can be ordered through the customer support system.

## Product Orders

Customers can ask about their product, order status, delivery status, and estimated delivery date.

## Product Support

If a customer has a problem with a product, customer support can provide information from the knowledge base or create a support ticket when necessary.

## Product Information Requests

Customers can ask questions about available products, product-related issues, and order-related information.

## Order Identification

Customers should provide their order ID when asking for specific order information. The system can use the order ID to retrieve information from the order database.
