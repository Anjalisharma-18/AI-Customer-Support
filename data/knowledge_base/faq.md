# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is the delivery time?

Standard orders are usually delivered within 5 to 7 business days.

## Can I cancel my order?

An order can be cancelled if it has not yet been shipped.

## How can I request a refund?

Customers can request a refund according to the company's refund policy.

## How can I contact customer support?

Customers can contact customer support through the AI customer support system. If the issue cannot be resolved, the conversation can be escalated to a human support representative.
