# Account Policy

## Account Information

Customers can contact support for help with account-related questions.

## Account Assistance

The support system can provide information from the knowledge base for common account questions.

## Account Problems

If an account issue cannot be resolved using the available information, a support ticket can be created for further assistance.

## Human Support

If the customer requests human assistance or the account problem remains unresolved, the conversation can be escalated to a human support representative.
