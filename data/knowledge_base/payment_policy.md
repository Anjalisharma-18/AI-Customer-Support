# Payment Policy

## Payment Methods

Customers can make payments using the payment methods supported by the company.

## Payment Status

Customers can ask about the status of a payment by providing their order ID or payment ID.

## Payment Information

The support system can retrieve payment information from the payment database when the required identifier is available.

## Failed Payment

If a payment fails, the customer can contact support for assistance.

## Payment Issues

If a payment problem cannot be resolved using the available information, the system can create a support ticket or escalate the conversation to a human support representative.
