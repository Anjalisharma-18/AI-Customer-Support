# Shipping Policy

## Standard Delivery

Standard orders are usually delivered within 5 to 7 business days.

## Delivery Status

Customers can ask the support system about the delivery status of their order by providing the order ID.

## Estimated Delivery

The system can provide the estimated delivery date when order information is available in the database.

## Tracking

If a tracking number is available for an order, customer support can provide the tracking information.

## Delayed Delivery

If an order is delayed or the customer has an unresolved delivery problem, a support ticket can be created or the conversation can be escalated to a human support representative.
